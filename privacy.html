RadScan Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

RadScan doesn't collect, sell or share your data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to the developer unless you email support yourself. This page says what the app does with information, in plain words. It matches the Privacy page inside the app (Settings › Privacy).

Your scanner or radio

Local network. RadScan talks directly to your scanner or Icom radio on your own network: its controls, its audio and (a scanner's) FTP server. Nothing passes through any other server. While it listens, RadScan tells other copies of RadScan on your network which scanner it's listening to, under this device's name, so one can take over the audio. That stays on your network.

Scanner FTP logins. Stored in your device's Keychain and used only to reach your scanner. FTP isn't encrypted, so use it on your own network.

Icom radio logins. The radio's network user is kept on your device, and its password in your device's Keychain. They're used only to log in to your radio, which is told a name for your device, like RadScan-1A2B. The radio's login isn't encrypted, so use it on your own network.

Location

RadScan uses your device's location only while the app is open: to show you on the map, once you've allowed it, and when you tap Move Scanner Here, to send it once to your scanner. It isn't stored or sent anywhere else, but for a broad area to place repeaters (below), and it's never read in the background.

Airports and weather transmitters on the map. They come with RadScan, from the FAA's and the National Weather Service's public lists. The map picks the ones near you on your device; nothing is looked up.

Ham repeaters on the map. To place repeaters, RadScan looks up their towns with Apple Maps and, for some, the callsign's public FCC license address at callook.info. Only town names, callsigns from your scanner's favorites or your CHIRP files and a broad search area (where you are to about 35 miles, or the area of your scanner's favorites) are sent. Results are kept on your device.

On your device

Recordings, backups and settings are kept on your device only. They leave it only when you share them.

Connection log. RadScan notes what happens between it and your scanner (connecting, audio starting and stopping, connection test results), kept on your device. It leaves only inside a support email you send from Help › Contact Support, where you choose what goes with it and can read it first.

Alerts are local notifications from your device. Nothing goes through a push server.

Purchases. The 5-day evaluation and buying RadScan are handled by Apple through the App Store. RadScan never sees your payment details.

Children

RadScan isn't directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
