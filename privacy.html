RadScan Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

RadScan doesn't collect, sell or share your data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to the developer. This page says what the app does with information, in plain words. It matches the Privacy page inside the app (Settings › Privacy).

Your scanner

Local network. RadScan talks directly to your scanner on your own network: its controls, its audio and its FTP server. Nothing passes through any other server.

Scanner FTP logins. Stored in your device's Keychain and used only to reach your scanner. FTP isn't encrypted, so use it on your own network.

Location

RadScan uses your device's location only while the app is open: to show you on the map, once you've allowed it, and when you tap Move Scanner Here, to send it once to your scanner. It isn't stored, it isn't sent anywhere else, and it's never read in the background.

Ham repeaters on the map. To place repeaters, RadScan looks up their towns with Apple Maps and, for some, the callsign's public FCC license address at callook.info. Only town names, callsigns from your scanner's favorites and a broad search area are sent. Results are kept on your device.

On your device

Recordings, backups and settings are kept on your device only. They leave it only when you share them.

Alerts are local notifications from your device. Nothing goes through a push server.

Purchases. The free trial and the unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. RadScan never sees your payment details.

Children

RadScan isn't directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
