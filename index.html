See the whole band at once.

A live picture of everything on a band. Tap a signal to tune to it, magnify to fine-tune, see station names, and save what you find.

Needs Uniden's Waterfall upgrade on the scanner. Try it any time on the Virtual Scanner.

And on your Mac.

On a Mac with Apple silicon, RadScan runs the same console in a window beside your work, with keyboard shortcuts in its Scanner menu: ⌘L to listen, ⌘← and ⌘→ for Prev and Next, ⌘↓ to Skip. Close the window and the scanner keeps playing; quit to stop.

No scanner handy?

The Virtual Scanner is an SDS200 simulated inside RadScan, with fictional channels and live audio. Every feature works on it, and it's always free.

Why one price.

I build the apps I want to use, and hope other people find them useful too. I'm not a fan of software sold as a subscription, or of apps that keep charging for the parts you actually use. So RadScan is one purchase, with everything in it.

What you need.

  • A Uniden SDS200 connected to your network by Ethernet.
  • An iPhone or iPad with iOS 18 or later, on the same network.
  • Or a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) with macOS 15 or later, on the same network. Intel-based Macs aren't supported.
  • An Android phone or tablet with Android 10 or later, on the same network.
  • For the waterfall: Uniden's Waterfall upgrade on the scanner.

Nothing to sign up for.

No account, no analytics, no tracking. RadScan doesn't collect anything about you. Read the privacy policy.
