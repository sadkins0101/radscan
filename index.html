No hub. No account. No cloud. Just your phone and your scanner.

RadScan talks straight to your SDS200 over your own network. Nothing passes through anyone else's server, and nothing about you is collected.

Run your scanner from the couch.

Listen anywhere in the house.

Stream your scanner's audio to iPhone or iPad, with the screen locked. Noise and hiss reduction, an equalizer and automatic leveling make rough channels easier to follow.

  • Lock Screen
  • Live Activity
  • AirPods
  • CarPlay
  • Noise reduction
  • Equalizer

Every key you reach for.

Hold on a system, department or channel. Skip, avoid, step back and forward. Set volume and squelch. Switch favorites lists, or change everything at once with scan profiles.

Every transmission, as it happens.

The Activity log shows each call with its talkgroup and unit ID. Get an alert when the channels you pick come on the air.

Record now.

Something worth keeping? Record now saves what you're hearing from the start of the call. Or record every transmission automatically.

Program it from your phone.

Add nearby ham repeaters and ready-made channel packs to your favorites. Back up your favorites and settings over the scanner's FTP server, and put them back.

  • Aircraft Common
  • FRS/GMRS
  • MURS
  • Business Itinerant
  • Interoperability
  • Ham repeaters

Waterfall

See the whole band at once.

A live picture of everything on a band. Tap a signal to tune to it, magnify to fine-tune, see station names, and save what you find.

Needs Uniden's Waterfall upgrade on the scanner. Try it any time on the Virtual Scanner.

Know who's on the air.

See where the traffic is coming from, and who is talking.

  • The map shows your sites, departments and nearby repeaters, lit up by what's on the air now and in the last few minutes.
  • Trunk Activity lists a trunked site's voice channels, talkgroups and units, live.

Made for iPad, too.

One console screen with scanning, the map, activity and recordings side by side, and keyboard shortcuts for the keys you use most. The waterfall and Trunk Activity get the whole screen.
