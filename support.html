RadScan Support

Remote control and live audio for the Uniden SDS200 and the Icom IC-705 and IC-9700, on iPhone, iPad and Mac (Apple silicon).

Start in the app: two steps to a fix

RadScan has its own support tools, under Help › Support. They get to the cause much faster than a description alone.

  1. Run the Connection Test (Help › Connection Test, or Settings › Connection Test while connected). In about 10 seconds it measures how quickly your scanner answers RadScan and how well its audio reaches your device: audio start, audio lost, longest audio gap, audio jitter, command response and commands unanswered. It rates the connection Excellent, Good, Fair or Poor, and says what might be wrong. The scanner keeps scanning while it runs.
  2. Write to us with Contact Support (Help › Contact Support). Tell us what's going wrong, and choose what goes with your email. Each item is a switch:
    • App and device: RadScan's version, your device and its system version.
    • Scanner: its model, its firmware, and whether RadScan is connected.
    • Last connection test: its rating and numbers.
    • Connection log: the most recent events between RadScan and your scanner (connecting, the audio starting and stopping, messages shown). It includes your scanner's and your device's addresses on your own network.
    Preview What's Sent shows exactly what's in it. Nothing leaves your device until you send the email yourself, and it goes only to radscan@enetworx.net. If Mail isn't set up, RadScan lets you share the report or copy it into an email instead.

You can also write to radscan@enetworx.net directly. Say which iPhone, iPad or Mac, its system version, and your scanner's firmware (Settings › Scanners). The app's own Help covers every screen in more detail.

What you need

  • A Uniden SDS200 connected to your network (Ethernet), or an Icom IC-705 (Wi-Fi) or IC-9700 (Ethernet), and your device on the same network.
  • An iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18 or later, or a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) with macOS 15 or later. Intel-based Macs aren't supported.
  • For the SDS200's waterfall: Uniden's Waterfall upgrade on the scanner.

RadScan can't find my scanner

  • Join the same network as the scanner.
  • Allow Local Network access. On iPhone and iPad: Settings › Apps › RadScan › Local Network. On a Mac: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.
  • Or add it by address: Settings › Scanners › Add Scanner. The scanner shows its address under Menu › Settings › LAN Settings.

Connecting an Icom radio

  • RadScan works with the Icom IC-705 and IC-9700. In the radio's network settings, turn on network control and set a network user and password.
  • In RadScan: Settings › Scanners › Add Scanner, and Search This Network on the Icom card. Tap your radio, enter that network user and password, and Connect to This Radio. Or use Add by IP Address, which is in the radio's network settings.
  • RadScan offers what your radio itself can do: its bands and modes, its spectrum scope and its settings. So features differ from radio to radio.
  • Only one app can use the radio over the network at a time. If it's busy, close the other one first.
  • RadScan only listens: it never makes the radio transmit. When you're done, it leaves the radio the way it found it. Radio Settings › Put Back as Found does it right away.
  • Nothing heard? Lower the squelch, check that RF gain is up (Radio Settings), and look at the channels it scans under the star.

No audio, or "Can't get the scanner's audio"

The SDS200 supports only one audio connection at a time. That's a limit of the scanner, not RadScan. If another phone, tablet or computer is listening, RadScan waits its turn and starts the audio as soon as it's free. If the other device has RadScan too, Listen says which one and offers to take over its audio.

If nothing else is listening, the scanner's audio server may be stuck. RadScan can restart the scanner to clear it (Settings › Audio › Reboot scanner if audio gets stuck), or you can restart it on the scanner itself. The Connection Test tells you whether another device has the audio.

RadScan on a Mac

  • It runs on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later) with macOS 15 or later. Intel-based Macs aren't supported.
  • Get it from the Mac App Store, where it's listed under iPhone & iPad Apps. Bought RadScan on your iPhone or iPad? The same purchase works on the Mac. If it doesn't show as purchased, use Settings › RadScan › Restore Purchase.
  • Closing the window keeps the scanner playing, as Music does. Click RadScan in the Dock to bring it back, and quit it (⌘Q) to stop.
  • The Scanner menu in the menu bar has the keyboard shortcuts: ⌘L to listen, ⌘← and ⌘→ for Prev and Next, ⌘↓ to Skip.
  • While RadScan waits its turn behind another device, keep it running until that device stops. Quitting then leaves the scanner's audio stuck until the scanner restarts.
  • Live Activities, CarPlay and the Lock Screen controls are for iPhone and iPad.

The waterfall says my scanner needs an upgrade

Every SDS200 has the waterfall built in, but Uniden keeps it switched off until the scanner has their Waterfall upgrade, entered on the scanner itself. RadScan has nothing to sell or unlock for it. You can try the waterfall on the Virtual Scanner.

Evaluating and buying RadScan

Every feature works with your own radio for 5 days, at no charge. After that, connecting to your radio needs RadScan bought once: no subscription. The virtual radios are always free. Bought it before, or on another device? Settings › RadScan › Restore Purchase.

Try it without a radio

The Virtual Scanner is an SDS200 simulated inside RadScan, with fictional channels and live audio. The Virtual Icom Radio is an IC-705 simulated the same way. Start either from the first screen or from Settings › Scanners › Add Scanner.
