RadScan for Android Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

RadScan doesn't collect, sell or share your data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to the developer. This page says what the app does with information, in plain words. It matches the Privacy page inside the app (Settings › Privacy).

Your scanner

Local network. RadScan talks directly to your scanner on your own network: its controls, its audio and its FTP server. Nothing passes through any other server.

Scanner FTP logins. Stored on your device, encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore, and used only to reach your scanner. FTP isn't encrypted, so use it on your own network.

Location

RadScan reads your device's location only when you tap Move Scanner Here, while the app is open: once, to show you on the map and to send it once to your scanner. It isn't stored, it isn't sent anywhere else, and it's never read in the background.

The map. The map's pictures come from OpenStreetMap. To draw them, your device asks OpenStreetMap's servers for the area you're looking at; they are kept on your device for a while so they needn't be fetched again.

Ham repeaters on the map. To place repeaters, RadScan looks up their towns with your device's own geocoding service (Google's, on most devices) and, for some, the callsign's public FCC license address at callook.info. Only town names, callsigns from your scanner's favorites and a broad search area are sent. Results are kept on your device.

On your device

Recordings, backups and settings are kept on your device only. They leave it only when you share them or save a copy somewhere yourself.

Alerts are local notifications from your device. Nothing goes through a push server.

Purchases. The unlock is handled by Google through Google Play. RadScan never sees your payment details. When your free trial began is kept on your device.

Children

RadScan isn't directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: radscan@enetworx.net.
