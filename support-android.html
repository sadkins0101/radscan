RadScan for Android Support

Remote control and live audio for the Uniden SDS200, on Android phones and tablets.

Questions, problems or ideas: radscan@enetworx.net. Say which phone or tablet, its Android version, and your scanner's firmware (RadScan shows it at the bottom of Settings while connected).

The app's own Help (Settings › Help) covers every screen in more detail.

What you need

  • A Uniden SDS200 connected to your network (Ethernet), and your phone or tablet on the same network.
  • Android 10 or later.
  • For the waterfall: Uniden's Waterfall upgrade on the scanner.

RadScan can't find my scanner

  • Join the same Wi-Fi network as the scanner.
  • Allow access to devices on your network when RadScan asks. If you didn't: Android Settings › Apps › RadScan › Permissions.
  • Or add it by address: Settings › Scanners › Add Scanner. The scanner shows its address under Menu › Settings › LAN Settings.

No audio, or "another device or app may be listening"

The SDS200 streams to one listener at a time. If another phone or app is listening, stop it there first. If nothing else is listening, the scanner's audio server may be stuck: RadScan can reboot the scanner to clear it (Settings › Audio › Reboot scanner if audio gets stuck), or reboot it from the scanner itself.

The waterfall says my scanner needs an upgrade

Every SDS200 has the waterfall built in, but Uniden keeps it switched off until the scanner has their Waterfall upgrade, entered on the scanner itself. RadScan has nothing to sell or unlock for it. You can try the waterfall on the Virtual Scanner.

Free trial and unlock

Every feature is free for 5 days. After that, connecting to your scanner needs a one-time unlock: no subscription. The Virtual Scanner is always free. Bought it before, or on another device with the same Google account? Settings › RadScan › Restore Purchase.

Try it without a scanner

The Virtual Scanner is an SDS200 simulated inside RadScan, with fictional channels and live audio. Start it from the first screen or from Settings › Scanners.
